About

Grounded in mechanics,
aimed upward.

I'm an aerospace engineer focused on the parts of a vehicle that have to survive contact with reality — load paths, thermal cycling, vibration, and the margins between an analysis and a test result.

Most recently I've worked on composite structures and small satellite propulsion, splitting time between simulation and the test bench, because neither one tells the whole story alone.
